Notes

Images have useful placeholders: [image: A mountain at dawn]
(mountain.png). Footnotes keep their labels and
content.[detail]

[detail] This is a terminal reader, so raw HTML and actual
         images are left to a browser.

Small details

Punctuation stays as written: "quotes", -- dashes, and ...
dots.
