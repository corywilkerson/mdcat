## Paragraphs inside items

1. Start with a short paragraph.

   Follow it with another paragraph that belongs to the same
   item, even when the text wraps onto another line.

   > A quoted reminder inside the item.
   > With [a reference](https://example.com).

2. Continue with the next item.

## Quotes inside quotes

> Outer quote with **emphasis** and `code`.
>
>> Inner quote, still inside the outer one.
>>
>> - A list belongs here too
>> - With a second item
>
> Back outside the inner quote.  
> This is an explicit new line.

## Numbering across nine

9. A long enough item to show how continuation lines line up
   with the beginning of the text, rather than its number.
10. The next item keeps the sequence.
